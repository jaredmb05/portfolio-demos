How the Leasing Desk works

Showsheet & floor plans

With Dropbox sync on, every Desk reads the showsheet and floor plan master from Dropbox and publishes them to the iPads when a file changes — edit the file in Dropbox, reload it there, then click Sync now. With sync off, use Upload .xlsx and Publish to iPads. A showsheet more than a day old is flagged on the card.

Tour Queue

  • Current Queue — who's waiting (with a wait estimate) and who's on a tour. Start and Finish tours here or on the iPads.
  • Follow-Ups to Send — finished tours; mark sent once the email is out.
  • Leasing Manager Check-In — the second touch, due the day after the follow-up.
  • Future Move-Ins — prospects who came in early; follow up 30 days before move-in.
  • Rotation and History — who's next, who's out, and everything shown in the last weeks (with Undo).

Email Builder — six templates

  • Tour Follow-Up and Inquiry Follow-Up — pick units from the list; each opens an editor for rent, concession, floor plan and a note. "No longer available" swaps in an apology and offers the selected units as alternatives.
  • Welcome Email — pick the building, copy the subject line; attach the COI.
  • PFA — Rental Info (lounges reservable on the 2nd & 4th weekend) or Payment Due (checks due 7 days after signing).
  • Application Confirmation and Lease Approval — fill the fields; the floor plan link follows the building and unit.

Then Copy for Outlook/Gmail and paste into a new email. If the email still has a blank like [Prospect], the Desk says so after copying.

Templates

Under Edit email templates you can change the Tour, Inquiry, Welcome and PFA emails, the "no longer available" opening, and the parking and shuttle blocks; changes are saved in this browser. Application Confirmation and Lease Approval aren't templates: they're built from their fields each time.
