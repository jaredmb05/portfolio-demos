# Open items

Engineering work that is deliberately left for after launch. Product features live in `BACKLOG.md`; fixes production also needs live in `PRODUCTION_PORT.md`.

## Post-launch
1. **Inline handlers → delegated listeners.** The HTML still calls about 290 functions by name from `on*` attributes (in `index.html` and in markup the render functions build), so each app's `main.js` puts those functions on `window`, plus accessors for the 11 variables handlers assign (D18). Replace them with `data-action` attributes and one delegated listener per app, then delete the `window` bridge. Needs the browser flows and the email byte-compare as the safety net; no behavior change intended.
2. **"¼" and "¾" in incentive text.** The iPad reads "½" as "1/2" (M2), but not "¼" or "¾": "¾ Month Free" on a unit with no showsheet net effective quotes the full rent. Both characters appear in the apps' own override menus. Extending the M2 rule is one line in `parseIncentive` plus tests; left out of the cleanup because the owner's ruling named only "½".
3. *(Superseded Oct 30, 2026: the owner chose to bring the demo up to production Desk v1.37 / iPad v1.29, including the v1.22–v1.23 tour times, the start-time question and the Firestore rules test; see `PORT_PLAN_V1.37.md`.)*
4. **SheetJS 0.18.5 has known advisories** (prototype pollution and a ReDoS when reading crafted files). It is the version production loads from cdnjs, now bundled here (`apps/shared/vendor/`) so the apps and the demo need no CDN. Fixed releases are published only on SheetJS's own CDN, not npm or cdnjs; upgrading is a change to the showsheet reader, so it needs the parser tests and the Desk flows.
5. **A typed rent with free months the parser can't read** ("6 Weeks Free", a dollar credit only): the Desk email leaves the net effective out (production D28), but the iPad still quotes the showsheet's NET EFF, which belongs to the old rent. Production behaves the same. The iPad should leave it out too; when the typed rent is below that NET EFF it even quotes a net above the rent ("$3,800" typed on a credit-only unit quotes $3,875). Reports never shows a net above the rent.
6. **A reopened tour whose unit is no longer on today's showsheet**, with an iPad rent change and no broker, still quotes "1 Month Free OR 1 Mo. OP" with no net, which mentions the OP to a prospect (D19). Production behaves the same; found while porting production v1.25.
7. **A lease term saved from the Desk's "Other concession" isn't read by the iPad.** The Desk email and Reports use the stop's saved term; the iPad quote (`resolveStop`) works the term out from the incentive text again. They differ only when someone typed a term that the incentive doesn't say.
8. **After a showsheet listener error, the iPad offers Reconnect anyway once the next re-check runs.** Production D44 shows no Reconnect after a listener error (a listener that errored can't be revived by turning the network off and on), but the 15-second or 60-second re-check calls the status again without the error and switches the banner to the plain Offline text with Reconnect. Ported as production has it; remembering the error in a flag would fix it.
9. **A tour with both a Max and price Flexible ticked** is left out of Reports' "Shown within the prospect's price" here, while production counts its Max. A tour with only Flexible is left out in both. Found while porting production v1.34; left as the demo's Reports model had it (group 8a) pending the owner's call.
10. **The ❄ snow button's color (#9fb3cc) is about 2:1 on white**, below the 3:1 a control needs. Production's color, kept as is; a darker tone is a one-line change in `apps/desk/styles.css`.
